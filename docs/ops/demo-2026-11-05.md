# Demo checklist (5 November 2026)

What works on this setup, how to start it, and how to show each part.
Measured on 4 November with the board on the iPhone hotspot.

## Start

1. iPhone: Personal Hotspot on, **Maximize Compatibility on** (2.4 GHz),
   phone plugged in. The board joins "iPhone (2)" by itself (saved on the board).
2. Board on USB. In the repo: `npm run dev`. The engine finds the board's
   COM port by itself (every port is tried every 2 s).
3. Open http://localhost:3000. The header shows **Live**.
4. **Always, shortly before showing it:** **Setup → Set up this room →
   Empty-room calibration → 2 minutes → Start**, PIN **2407**, everyone out
   for 2 minutes. The room's background drifts within an hour, and moving
   the board or the phone makes the old calibration wrong.

## What to show

| Part | How | What appears |
|---|---|---|
| Presence | Walk in from outside | "Nobody in the room" → "Someone is home" in a few seconds |
| Movement | Walk around | The 3D view and signals chart ripple ("3D view and live signals") |
| Breathing | Sit still about 1 m from the board, between board and phone. Wait 20 s, then breathe **slowly and deeply** (about 12 a minute: 2.5 s in, 2.5 s out) for at least 30 s | A breaths/min number in the Vital signs card. It may come and go |
| Breathing pace helper | Open http://localhost:8765/.claude/pacer.html after `python -m http.server 8765` in the repo | A circle to breathe with (`?bpm=12` sets the pace) |
| Alerts | Unplug the board's USB | "Can't read the sensor board", then a "sensor offline" alert; plug back in and it recovers |

## Limits to say out loud

- **One person alone.** A second person in the room mixes into the signal.
- **Calm, normal breathing is not picked up** with the phone as the Wi-Fi
  source: it was below the noise in the 4 November check. Slow, deep breaths
  are. The app shows nothing rather than a wrong number.
- **Heart rate does not work on this setup** (experimental; it stays at
  "signal too weak"). A heartbeat moves the chest about ten times less than a breath.
- **Fall alarm is a demo feature, not validated.** It needs a hard fall, then
  lying completely still for 3+ s, while the dashboard shows "Someone is home".
- Not a medical device.

## What was changed for this (4 November)

- `engine/config/rules.yaml` → `breathing`: the shown rate uses the gates a
  held-out test on the public E1 set picked (confidence 0.5, ratio 1.0), a
  stillness bar for vital signs 3× presence's (deep breaths moved the
  shoulders past presence's very low bar), and a 20 s hold of the last good
  rate. The breathing-stopped alarm keeps its validated settings.
- `presence.b_on_x` 1.0 → 1.5: the empty room twice turned "Someone is home"
  on breathing energy alone in 1.5 h; on the public E4 set 1.5 flags no empty
  room. Walking in is found by movement either way.
- `lifefi/presence.py`: someone found without movement was marked "Not sure if
  anyone is there" after 5 s instead of 10 min (the timer counted from a motion
  before they came in). Fixed, with a test.
- Checked on the board: the deep 12 /min check showed the right rate
  (11 /min), the calm 15 /min check and 5 minutes of empty room showed nothing,
  and no heart value appeared in either.
- Recordings: `data/sessions/breathcheck-20261104-181251` (deep, 12 /min),
  `calm15-20261104-182838` (calm, 15 /min), `empty-20261104-182147` (empty room).
