# Gemini / Google-stack options for Life-Fi (no budget, no key yet)

Checked 2026-10-28. Context: EduHack Plovdiv 2026 (final 2026-11-05), "technological execution: how effectively the Google stack is integrated" = 25 % of the score. Our use is tiny: a Node/TypeScript server sends a short text summary of already-computed day data (presence, posture, breathing rate, alerts; no raw signals) and asks for 2–3 plain-language sentences for a caregiver, plus "explain this alert". A few calls per day, a few hundred during rehearsals.

## 1. Google AI Studio / Gemini API free tier

- **Free models today** (pricing page, "Free of charge" input + output): Gemini 3.8 Flash, 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, 3.1 Flash-Lite, the 2.5 family (Pro/Flash/Flash-Lite, restricted for new projects), Gemini Embedding 2 and **Gemma 4** (served through the same API). Not free: Gemini 4 Argon, 3.1 Pro, image/video models. Batch/Flex are paid-only. Source: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing
- **Limits**: Google no longer publishes per-model free-tier RPM/RPD; they are shown per project at https://aistudio.google.com/rate-limit. Community measurements (indicative, Sept–Oct 2026): Flash-Lite about 10–15 RPM and ~500 requests/day; 3.8/3.6 Flash as low as ~20 requests/day. Free tier has no billing fallback: over the limit, calls fail with 429 instead of costing money. Sources: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/rate-limits , https://klymentiev.com/blog/gemini-api-free-tier , https://www.scriptbyai.com/gemini-api-free-tier-limits/
- **Card / billing**: none needed for the free tier. Paid tier = link a Cloud Billing account and **prepay at least $5** (new accounts default to prepay). Source: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/billing
- **Availability**: Bulgaria is on the available-regions list; AI Studio is free in all available regions. Source: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/available-regions
- **Account type**: personal Google account works (terms require 18+). Google Workspace (incl. Workspace for Education) has AI Studio on by default but an admin can switch it off, and **users under 18 on Education accounts cannot use it**. Source: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/workspace
- **Terms (Gemini API Additional Terms, last updated 2026-04-28)**, https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms :
  - *Unpaid Services* (free quota, AI Studio): Google uses content "to provide, improve, and develop" products; "human reviewers may read, annotate, and process your API input and output"; "Do not submit sensitive, confidential, or personal information to the Unpaid Services."
  - *Paid Services* (project linked to an active billing account): "Google doesn't use your prompts ... or responses to improve our products"; prompts logged only briefly for abuse detection.
  - **EEA/UK/Switzerland clause**: "If you're in the European Economic Area, Switzerland, or the United Kingdom, the terms under 'How Google uses Your Data' in 'Paid Services' apply to all Services, including Google AI Studio and unpaid quota in the Gemini API, even though they are offered free of charge." Whether this is keyed to the account's country or the request IP is not stated (open forum question: https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/clarification-on-eea-data-training-policy-trigger-account-entity-vs-ip-geolocation/179774 ).
  - Use restriction: "You may use only Paid Services when making API Clients available to users in the European Economic Area, Switzerland, or the United Kingdom." A hackathon prototype demoed by the team is not an API client offered to the public, but a real EU product would need the paid tier.
  - Also: not for "clinical practice" or "medical advice"; health content is informational only.
- **Privacy verdict for our summary** ("resident was in bed 7 h, breathing 14/min, one fall at 14:02 acknowledged"): acceptable on the free tier **if it carries no identifier** (no name, room number tied to a person, date of birth, address, device serial, contact details). With a Bulgarian account the EEA clause should already give paid-tier data terms, but do not rely on it alone: (a) strip identifiers server-side before the call (use "the resident" / "room A"), (b) send only derived values, never raw signal windows, (c) label the output as a summary, not medical advice, (d) for anything beyond the demo, use a paid key or Vertex AI in an EU region.

## 2. Google Cloud free trial (Vertex AI)

- "$300 Welcome credit to spend over 90 days"; requires "a credit card or other payment method" (authorisation hold, not a charge); no automatic charge at trial end (upgrade is manual, resources stop otherwise). Eligibility: never a paying Cloud/Maps/Firebase user and no previous trial. A student with a personal account and a card can take it; no company is needed. Source: https://docs.cloud.google.com/free/docs/free-cloud-features
- The credit **cannot pay for Gemini API in AI Studio** but can pay for Gemini on Vertex AI (first-party models; only partner models are excluded). There is no Vertex AI "always free" tier.
- **EU data residency**: Vertex AI (now "Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform") offers `eu` multi-region (`aiplatform.eu.rep.googleapis.com`) and EU regions (europe-west1 Belgium, europe-west3 Frankfurt, europe-west4 Netherlands, europe-west8 Milan, europe-west9 Paris, europe-central2 Warsaw, ...). The `eu` endpoint "strictly covers data residency within EU member states" for ML processing; the `global` endpoint gives no residency guarantee. Flash and Flash-Lite 3.x models are all available in EU regions. Sources: https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/learn/locations , https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/learn/data-residency

## 3. Programs that could give free credits

- **EduHack itself (best lead)**: eduhack.bg states that Build-with-AI teams "receive Google Cloud and Vertex AI credits plus free access to Google Skills", and the universities category lists "Access to the Gemini API, Flutter and Firebase AI Logic". The page does not say how the credits are handed out (no form or coupon mentioned); the GDG Plovdiv page does not mention them either. Ask the organisers / mentors directly. Sources: https://eduhack.bg/en/ , https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-plovdiv/
- **Google for Startups Cloud Program**: for companies (founded <10 years, tiers from "Start" for bootstrapped founders to $200k–$350k for VC-funded; must not have received >$5,000 in credits before). Not for a student team without a legal entity. Sources: https://cloud.google.com/startup , https://cloud.google.com/startup/ai
- **Google Cloud for Education**: teaching credits are requested by **faculty** with an edu email (https://edu.google.com/programs/credits/teaching/); students get them via a course. A TU Sofia–Plovdiv lecturer could apply, but not in time for 2026-11-05. Source: https://cloud.google.com/education
- **Gemini student offer**: free 1-year Google AI Plus (Bulgarian page; 18+, claim by 2026-12-31, payment method required, then €4.99/month unless cancelled). Covers the Gemini app, higher limits, 400 GB storage. **No API / AI Studio credits.** Source: https://gemini.google/students/

## 4. Firebase AI Logic

- Client SDKs (Web/JS, Flutter, Android, iOS, Unity) + a Google-run proxy that keeps the Gemini key off the client; Firebase App Check limits abuse. A web app can call Gemini **without its own backend**. Backend choice: Gemini Developer API (free tier, Spark plan, "lets you get started without having to provide a payment method") or Vertex AI / Agent Platform (needs Blaze plan + billing; lets you pick an EU location). The SDK itself is free; model usage follows Gemini API pricing. Sources: https://firebase.google.com/docs/ai-logic , https://firebase.google.com/docs/ai-logic/pricing , https://firebase.google.com/docs/ai-logic/locations
- Jury value: it is one of the tools named in the brief, so "Gemini via Firebase AI Logic" reads as deeper Google-stack use than a bare REST call. Cost: a Firebase project, and our summaries would be generated in the browser instead of on the server where the data already lives. For our server-side use, the plain Gemini API (`@google/genai`) is the honest fit; Firebase AI Logic is worth it only if we want "explain this alert" directly in the UI.

## 5. Fallbacks that work with no key and no internet

- **Template generator on the server** (must exist anyway): deterministic sentences built from the same summary object ("Maria's resident spent 7 h in bed, breathing stayed in the normal range (14/min). One fall at 14:02 was acknowledged by the carer at 14:05."). Used when no key is configured, when the API returns 429/5xx, or when the model answer fails a sanity check (no numbers that are not in the input).
- **Gemma locally through Ollama** (a Google open model; Gemma 4 and Gemma 3 are also served free on the Gemini API, so the same prompt works in both places):
  - `gemma3:1b` 806 MB download, 32K context, text-only; runs on any laptop, ~10 tok/s even on a Raspberry Pi 5 CPU. `gemma3:4b` 3.4 GB, ~3–4 tok/s on Pi-class CPUs, ~15 tok/s on an 8 GB M1. Sources: https://ollama.com/library/gemma3 , https://www.kunalganglani.com/blog/gemma-3-raspberry-pi-5-benchmark.md
  - `gemma4:e2b` 4.6–7.5 GB, `gemma4:e4b` 6.6–9.5 GB, 128K context; weights at 4-bit ~2.9 GB (E2B) / ~4.5 GB (E4B) plus KV cache, so 8 GB RAM is the practical minimum, 16 GB comfortable; community estimates 5–15 tok/s CPU-only. Sources: https://ollama.com/library/gemma4 , https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/core
  - A 2–3 sentence answer is ~60–80 tokens: 5–15 s on CPU with E2B, ~3 s with gemma3:1b. Ollama exposes http://localhost:11434 (native + OpenAI-compatible API); Ollama >= 0.20 for Gemma 4. Pull the model before the demo; no internet needed afterwards.

## Recommendation

Cheapest honest path: (1) today, ask the EduHack / GDG Plovdiv mentors how the promised Google Cloud + Vertex AI credits are issued, since that is the only route to a paid-terms, EU-resident setup at zero cost; (2) in parallel, create a **free Gemini API key** with a personal Google account of a team member who is 18+ (not a school Workspace account) at https://aistudio.google.com/apikey , use `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` (or `gemini-3.8-flash` for the handful of "explain this alert" calls), and keep the prompt identifier-free (no names, room labels only, derived values only; this is also what we tell the jury); (3) ship the template generator as the default and Gemma via Ollama as the offline "Google model" fallback, so the demo never depends on Wi-Fi or quota. Key handling: the key goes in `server/.env` as `GEMINI_API_KEY=` (the root `.gitignore` already ignores `.env` and `.env.*`), with an empty placeholder in `server/.env.example`; if the key is ever pasted in chat, a screenshot or a commit, delete it in AI Studio and create a new one. If credits arrive, switch the same code to Vertex AI with `location: "europe-west4"` (or the `eu` endpoint) and say so on the slide.
