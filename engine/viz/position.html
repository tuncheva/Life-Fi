Model

d = 10(A − R)/(10·n), where R is how strongly the body changes the subcarriers (dB re 1 dB²). A and n are guesses until you calibrate. Movement also raises R, so moving looks closer: calibrate sitting still.

Calibrate

Pause where you know the true distance, enter it and add the point. One point fits A; two or more fit A and n.
